## Exercise 3: Simple Budget

A student has `5000 FCFA`.

They spend:

- `1200 FCFA` on food
- `800 FCFA` on transport

Write a program that calculates:

1. Total amount spent
2. Remaining money
3. Whether they still have at least `3000 FCFA`
